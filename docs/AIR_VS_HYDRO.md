# FREEAXE 21 Air vs Hydro — Run 19

New public BM1373 evidence materially lowers the expected power band at the 20 TH/s target.
Naja Duo is reported at 4.4 TH/s and 42 W for two BM1373s (~9.55 J/TH). A four-chip Nexus
S1-4 is commercially published around 10 TH/s at 100-130 W (~10-13 J/TH). These are external
systems, not FREEAXE measurements.

For eight chips, 20 TH/s therefore maps to roughly 191 W at 9.55 J/TH and 260 W at 13 J/TH.
FREEAXE Air retains 20 TH/s as its certification target and a 400 W thermal qualification
envelope, giving substantial modeled margin without claiming that the board will actually
consume 400 W.

Hydro retains 20.6 TH/s as the current sustained-design estimate. At 9.55-13 J/TH it maps to
roughly 197-268 W. A 30 TH/s Hydro point is still experimental: 390 W at 13 J/TH and 450 W
at 15 J/TH, the latter exceeding the current 400 W thermal envelope.

The comparison therefore does not raise either sustained rating. Hydro has more thermal
headroom, while Air remains mechanically simpler. Physical FREEAXE power, temperature,
accepted-share and long-soak data are required before either rating can be certified.
